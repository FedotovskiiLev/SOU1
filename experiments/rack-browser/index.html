One shared MIDI composer. Recorded electronic and acoustic drums. A bounded local listening bench.

Prototype, not the production app. Prepares up to 30 seconds in a worker, then plays a finite buffer. No microphone, accounts, uploads or background playback. Start quietly.

All forms include a 2-second release tail within the 30-second limit. Guigui dynamic switches to authored hard hits above velocity 0.6. Try drum velocity scale 1.6 for stronger accents; softer scores may sound identical to the soft kit. Lower render gain if a peak is rejected.
